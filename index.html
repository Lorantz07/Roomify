How it works

From empty plan to a room that works.

  1. 01

    Create your room

    Enter the real width and depth in centimeters. Choose a floor and wall finish, and your measured plan is ready.

  2. 02

    Add your furniture

    Bring in beds, sofas, tables, and storage from a considered library. Every piece arrives at its true size.

  3. 03

    Make it yours

    Rearrange, rotate, resize, and recolor. Step back, live with the idea, and change it again whenever you like.
